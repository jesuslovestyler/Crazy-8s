How to play

Each player is dealt 7 cards. One card starts the discard pile; the rest is the draw stock.

  • On your turn, play a card that matches the top card's suit or rank.
  • An 8 is wild — it can be played on anything, and whoever plays it chooses the new active suit. ♥ ♦ ♣ ♠
  • No matching card? Tap the draw stock and keep drawing until a playable card turns up. You may play that card, or Pass and keep it. If the stock runs out, the discard pile is reshuffled into a new stock.
  • The first player to empty their hand wins the round and scores the value of the cards left in the other hand: 8s count 50, face cards 10, aces 1, and every other card its face value.
  • The round winner leads the next round. The first player to 250 points wins the match.

In 2 Players mode, pass the device back and forth — a handoff screen keeps each hand private until its owner taps to reveal it.

A friendly game of cards — no stakes, just the win.
